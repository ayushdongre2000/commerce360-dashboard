About this demo and how the figures are calculated

All orders, costs, production batches, inventory movements and advertising figures are synthetic. This is a fictional enterprise e-commerce business covering 1 October 2021 to 30 September 2026. It is not actual Amazon or Flipkart data or a past client project. Channel labels are examples of simulated sales channels. Filters and report exports are functional; no external business systems are connected.

Net sales are recognised on simulated dispatched orders and reduced by returned-order refunds. Actual operating costs include recognised product costs, packaging, delivery, channel fees, overhead, return handling and simulated advertising spend. Operating profit excludes taxes, interest and capital expenditure. Advertising return is net attributed revenue, not incremental profit. ROAS is attributed revenue divided by spend.

Date filters select orders by order date, ads by campaign day and production by ledger day. Batch delay counts use finish dates. Stock as of the end date is opening stock + production + restocked returns − shipped units. Production and inventory are shared across sales channels; category filters apply. Late/at-risk orders are counted once even when both dispatch and delivery were late. This is a demonstration of reporting behaviour rather than an audited financial statement.
